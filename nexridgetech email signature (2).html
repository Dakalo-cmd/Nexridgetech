NexRidgeTech — Email Signature Generator

Fill in each person's details, then copy the signature straight into Gmail or Outlook's signature settings.

How to install it
  1. Fill in your name, title, phone and email above.
  2. Click Copy signature.
  3. Gmail: Settings → See all settings → Signature → New signature → paste (Ctrl/Cmd+V).
  4. Outlook: Settings → Mail → Compose and reply → Signature → paste into the box.
