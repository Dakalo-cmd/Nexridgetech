1. Introduction

NexridgeTech IT Solutions ("NexridgeTech", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information of everyone who visits our website or uses our services. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and what rights you have in relation to it.

This Policy is drafted in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA"), the South African law that governs the lawful processing of personal information. By using our website at nexridgetech.co.za (the "Website") or otherwise providing us with your information, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy.

3. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that is necessary for the purposes described in this Policy. We collect information in the following ways:

3.1 Information you give us directly

When you submit an enquiry through our contact form, we collect:

  • Your first and last name
  • Your email address
  • The type of project or service you are enquiring about
  • Any project details, requirements, or messages you choose to include

If you contact us directly by phone, email, or WhatsApp, we collect the information you choose to share with us during that communication, which may include your name, phone number, and the content of your messages.

3.2 Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our Website automatically collects certain technical information when you visit, including your IP address, browser type and version, device type, operating system, pages viewed, referring website, and the date and time of your visit. We collect this information through Google Analytics, as described in the Cookies section below.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the personal information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide you with quotes or proposals
  • To communicate with you about your project, including scheduling, updates, and support
  • To deliver, maintain, and improve our web development, IT support, and networking services
  • To understand how visitors use our Website, so that we can improve its content, performance, and user experience
  • To comply with our legal, accounting, and regulatory obligations
  • To protect the security and integrity of our Website and business systems

We do not use your personal information for any purpose that is incompatible with the purpose for which it was originally collected, without first obtaining your consent where required by law.

5. Our Legal Basis for Processing

POPIA requires that personal information only be processed where a lawful justification exists. We rely on the following grounds:

  • Consent — where you voluntarily submit information to us, for example through our contact form, or agree to cookies on our Website
  • Performance of a contract — where processing is necessary to prepare a quote, deliver a project, or perform a service you have engaged us for
  • Legitimate interest — where processing helps us operate, secure, and improve our business and Website, provided this does not unreasonably override your own privacy interests
  • Legal obligation — where we are required to process or retain information to comply with South African law

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our Website uses Google Analytics to help us understand how visitors interact with our site. Google Analytics uses cookies — small text files stored on your device — to collect information such as pages visited, time spent on the site, and general location (derived from IP address).

This information is processed by Google in accordance with Google's own Privacy Policy. We do not use this data to personally identify you.

You can control or disable cookies at any time through your browser settings, or by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. Please note that disabling cookies may affect certain features of our Website.

Some of the third-party service providers listed above (including Google and Meta) may process or store information on servers located outside South Africa. Where this occurs, we take reasonable steps to ensure that such transfers are subject to appropriate safeguards consistent with the requirements of POPIA, including relying on service providers that are themselves subject to comparable data protection obligations.

9. How Long We Keep Your Information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, including:

  • Enquiry and project-related information — for the duration of our business relationship, and thereafter for a reasonable period to handle any follow-up support, warranty, or legal matters
  • Website analytics data — as retained by Google Analytics in accordance with its own data retention settings
  • Financial and accounting records — for the period required under South African tax and financial record-keeping legislation

Once information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to securely delete, destroy, or de-identify it.

10. How We Protect Your Information

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the personal information in our possession against loss, unauthorised access, alteration, or disclosure. These measures include restricting access to personal information to those who need it, using secure communication channels, and working with reputable third-party service providers.

While we take reasonable steps to secure your information, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Your Rights Under POPIA

As a data subject, POPIA gives you the right to:

  • Be notified that your personal information is being collected, and why
  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request that we correct, update, or delete inaccurate, irrelevant, excessive, or unlawfully obtained personal information
  • Object to the processing of your personal information, on reasonable grounds
  • Withdraw your consent to processing at any time, where processing is based on consent (this does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal)
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator if you believe your rights under POPIA have been infringed

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in Section 15 below. We will respond to your request within a reasonable time and in accordance with our obligations under POPIA.

12. Children's Privacy

Our Website and services are directed at businesses and are not intended for use by children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can take appropriate steps to remove it.

13. Links to Other Websites

Our Website may contain links to third-party websites, including social media platforms and service providers referenced in this Policy. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any third-party website you visit.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this Policy periodically.
