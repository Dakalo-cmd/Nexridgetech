FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers to the questions businesses ask most.

How long will it take to complete my project?

Standard business websites typically take 5–14 days. More advanced systems and networking projects are scoped with clear timelines before work begins.

Do you handle hosting, domains and maintenance?

Yes. We manage hosting, domains, SSL certificates, security and ongoing maintenance so you can focus on your business.

Can you upgrade my existing website?

Absolutely. We first assess your current website and determine whether an upgrade or a complete rebuild will provide the best value.

What do you need from me to get started?

Usually your business information, logo, preferred colours and a brief discussion about your goals.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. All project costs are discussed and agreed upon before work begins.

What support do you provide after launch?

Every project includes post-launch support, and we also offer ongoing maintenance and IT support packages.

Who works on my project?

You work directly with the developer and technical team building your solution.
